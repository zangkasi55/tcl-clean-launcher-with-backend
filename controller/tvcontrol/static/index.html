    THE DAILY RULE

    You set the boundary.

    Time on screen counts, including menus and paused videos. Visits add up. Reopening an exhausted app sends the TV back to standby.

    A timer, not a tamper-proof lock. The NAS and authorized ADB connection must remain online. Only the selected app is limited, regardless of who is watching. Other apps remain available. Offline gaps are not charged or presented as successful enforcement.

    No app password is required in this LAN-only deployment. Any allowed device on your home network can change these settings.

    STAGED SETUP & RECOVERY

    Always a way back.

    Install and open the clean launcher first. Choose it as Home only after testing your apps and remote.

    True default disables only the two competing Google TV Home packages; both remain installed for recovery. Restore original Home re-enables them. Automatic failback requires the NAS and authorized ADB; it handles a missing launcher, three crashes in two minutes, or an Android ANR. Compatibility mode leaves all apps enabled.
